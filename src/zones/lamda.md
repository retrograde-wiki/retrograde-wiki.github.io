**ZONE 11 (LAMDA)** is a meticulously maintained district owned and operated by [BioMedica](/monoliths/biomedica/). It is known for its elegant architecture, extensive greenery, and high quality of life. Lamda is home to thousands of corporate and medical employees, and the district has a reputation for being comfortable, expensive, and snobbish.

# Geography & Infrastructure

The district is characterized by elegant, Art Deco-inspired architecture and an unusually large amount of greenery. Gardens, vines, and landscaped public spaces are all throughout the streets and buildings, as well as air purifiers.

[BioMedica](/monoliths/biomedica/) maintains much of the districts infrastructure. The air is filtered, security cameras are very common, and public spaces are cleaned and maintained regularly. Housing in the district is expensive but through employment, employees can gain housing fairly easily. Because of this, a most of the middle class workers in this district work for [BioMedica](/monoliths/biomedica/), whether its in the offices, or as a paramedic.

Because of the quality of life, There is a large sub-districts for Resorts, vacationing, and Retirement for the older rich folks. 
# Culture

Lamdans are stereotyped as Old, Wealthy, Snobby, Sheltered, and Germophobic. residents are percieved as having easier, safer lives and are **easy targets for robberies** and murder.

Most residents work in office, corporate, medical or research jobs. Because of this, the zone **receives less Fixer activity** then other zones.

The district is also home to a large retirement population. Many older residents move here specifically for its safety, high-quality healthcare, clean environment, and comfort. A **significant portion** of the district is dedicated to retirement communities and services for elderly residents. 

Life here is generally comfortable, but also somewhat insular. Many residents live, work, receive healthcare, and socialize entirely within the district.
