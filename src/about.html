RETROGRADE

700 Years ago, Planet Earth was left by humanity to rot after its veins were destroyed by climate change and pollution. Humanity took to the stars--Before discovering and occupying their new home planet, Zenith.

Zenith was a lot like earth in many ways, and completely alien in others. Among these differences are Anomalies-- Beings not understood by science who warp reality in strange ways. An Anomaly can range from your local shop turning into a flesh pit or a 10 foot spider-like creature requiring an entire militia to take it down.

Due to the danger Anomalies and Environment possess, 24 ZONES were constructed to house the people of Zenith. Each ruled by a Monolith-- a Corporation that has grown so powerful it can control a what is effectively a country. , Who seem intent on keeping life in Zenith as difficult as possible for its inhabitants.

Between said ZONES is the Pale, uninhabitable toxic wastelands reserved for Anomalies and those too poor to afford living in a zone. Leaving your zone is thought of as a death sentence, so people mainly traverse by taking the Metro, a train that spans every zone.

So, You dropped out of your corpo-college and want to make it big on your own, How are you supposed to get by? Many would become a Fixer, self-employed mercenaries who solve problems for other people. This can range from running illegal-cargo between zones through the, to assassination, to handling peoples taxes. Or, you could join a Corporation like the rest of your friends... Though we cant promise it would be much safer.

Most likely, you'll either die from overworking yourself for your corporate overlords, Die in a fight as a Fixer, or Die as an Anomalies meal. Your choice!
