# Overview

**BioMedica** is Zenith's largest healthcare and medical corporation, and the **Monolith** for ZONE 11 // LAMDA

The company is well known for its healthcare plans, especially for employees of the world's various Monoliths. BioMedica offers highly competitive insurance and medical benefits to Monolith employees, **making working at a Monolith a much more attractive offer**.

BioMedica also manufactures a **wide range of medical products**, including its famous Healing Foam. While pricey for the average person, its commonly used by [Fixers](/lore/fixers/) who need a quick fix on the job to keep moving. 

The corporation employs an enormous number of people across its medical, administrative, research, manufacturing, and corporate divisions, and it has hospitals in each district. BioMedica, even among Monoliths, is particularly all encompassing. **There is no other option** if you need healthcare.

Most of BioMedica's public-facing operations are widely considered legitimate and effective. Its hospitals are well-funded, its research has produced major medical advancement.

## PROJECT MANDRAKE

**Project Mandrake** is a highly, highly classified research operation involving the cultivation of medicinal plant life known as Mandrakes. **Mandrakes are the key ingredient to BioMedica's medical technology.**

For the cultivation of Mandrakes, it requires a Human Host who is **alive but severely incapacitated**. BioMedica operatives scout hospitals for coma patients, terminally ill individuals, Particularly those thought to have low chance of recovery.


Families are informed there is no hope for survival, before the patient is transferred into BioMedica's Custody (For 'Scientific Research', a clause agreed to if you use their healthcare).

![Image start. A naked man lying on a medical table, hooked to multiple iv's and a breathing mask. He has large, unnatural plants with white buds pouring out of his stomach and eyesockets. Image end](/im/art/mandrake_host.png)

The subjects are kept alive through extensive life-support systems and maintained in a medically induced comas while The Mandrake's are grow within their bodies, sprouting through their stomach, eyes, and other orifices. The plants are then harvested for medicinal use.


On rare occasions, a host may regain consciousness.  incidents are typically violent and extremely difficult to contain. It is unknown during these incidents if the Mandrake or Host is the one in control

## PROJECT AMARANTH

**Project Amaranth** is BioMedica's classified longevity research program.

The project originated from research conducted within BioMedica's retirement communities, where researchers discovered unusual biological traits among a small number of exceptionally long-lived residents. Further research eventually led to the development of an experimental treatment capable of significantly reversing biological aging.

The treatment is not publicly available. It is instead quietly provided to an **extremely exclusive** clientele, including wealthy individuals, powerful corporate figures, and Monolith owners.

# Structure
While the company's exact internal structure is not publicly known, its operations are broadly divided between several major branches:

- Medical Services
- Insurance & Benufacturers
- Research & Development
- Manufacturing
- Administration

# Relationships

- **OTHER MONOLITHS** — BioMedica maintains close relationships with many of the world's Monoliths by healthcare and insurance programs to their employees.
- **THE PUBLIC** — Public perception of LAMDA is generally neutral/positive, especially compared to other monoliths. Criticism usually focuses on the corporation's pricing.
