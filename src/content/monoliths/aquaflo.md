## OVERVIEW

The water of Zenith is mildly toxic. It can be safely consumed for short periods, but long-term exposure causes a variety of health problems and can significantly reduce a person's lifespan.

AquaFlo is the Monolith responsible for extracting, purifying, and distributing water across Zenith. Its infrastructure includes wells, treatment plants, pipelines, reservoirs, and countless smaller facilities scattered throughout the Zones and Pale.

AquaFlo is particularly prominent in Rho, where centuries of water extraction have made the company a major part of the Zone's economy and development.

## HISTORY

AquaFlo was established during the formation of the modern Zones, originally to deal with Zenith's contaminated water. Its early infrastructure was concentrated in what would become Rho, where the company quickly became responsible for much of the region's water supply.

As AquaFlo expanded its purification plants and extraction sites, its control over Rho's water infrastructure eventually allowed it to become the Zone's Monolith.

The company continued expanding across Zenith over the following centuries. Some of its older water sources have since been exhausted, leaving abandoned facilities scattered throughout Rho. Many have been salvaged or repurposed by locals, while others remain under AquaFlo ownership.

AquaFlo's leadership has remained within the same family for generations. 

## CULTURE

AquaFlo has a strong sense of hierarchy. The company's ruling family is treated with considerably more respect and ceremony than ordinary employees, and differences in wealth and living conditions are obvious.

The company takes considerable pride in its history and presents water management as a responsibility passed down through generations. Many employees genuinely take pride in keeping their communities supplied with clean water, while others are simply there for the stable employment and benefits.

Working conditions vary depending on the job. Office positions and specialized careers can be comfortable, while factory, maintenance, and field work can be physically demanding and dangerous.

AquaFlo employees often develop strong camaraderie, particularly among workers who spend long periods together at remote facilities or company towns.


## WORKING FOR AQUAFLO

An employee working at Aquaflo might work at a purification plant, travel between monoliths repairing pipelines, or provide security for an isolated facility.

More *interestingly*, many employees are being forced to set out on expeditions to survey The Pale for water sources. Working in and exploring the Pale is dangerous, but positions may come with better pay or benefits. 

Some employees spend years moving between facilities as the company follows new water sources.

## RUMORS
AquaFlo has kept Zenithians satiated for generations, so many try to not push their luck with the corp. In Rho, where the company's operations have reshaped the land for generations, people have more reasons to be suspicious.

- **The Border**: Rho's outskirts seem to change every few years. New extraction sites appear farther into the wasteland, followed by roads, settlements, and company facilities. Some Rhoans wonder how much land the company intends to claim.
- **No Such Thing as Abandoned**: Old AquaFlo facilities are scattered throughout Rho, some left behind when their water sources ran dry. There are stories of surveyors returning to these sites years later, taking measurements and marking the surrounding land for unknown reasons.
- **Displacement**: Talora communities have been pushed out of areas earmarked for AquaFlo projects. The company insists its operations are lawful, but locals tell stories of settlements abandoned under pressure, sometimes before any official announcement of construction.

## NOTES

- AquaFlo CEO is historically titled **Lord/Lady [NAME], the Great Purifier of the Waters**. The full title still appears on official documents, although most people simply call them the CEO.
- AquaFlo is one of the oldest continuously operating Monoliths.
- It is one of the most geographically spread-out Monoliths, with facilities throughout the Zones and Pale.
- AquaFlo infrastructure can remain operational for generations, with newer systems often built around older facilities.
- Many Rho settlements originally developed around AquaFlo infrastructure.
