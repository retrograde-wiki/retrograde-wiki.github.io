## OVERVIEW

Rho is a sparsely populated Zone ravaged by its water industry and [Monolith](/lore/companies.html) [AquaFlo](/monoliths/aquaflo.html). Much of the Zone consists of dry plains, red sandstone, deep canyons, and mesas. Most settlements are small and separated by long stretches of undeveloped land.

Water extraction has been one of Rho's defining industries for centuries. The Zone is an important source of water for the rest of [Zenith](/lore/zenith.html), although the wealth generated by the industry is not evenly distributed.

## GEOGRAPHY & INFRASTRUCTURE

Rho's terrain is mostly dry and sun-baked, with sparse vegetation outside areas near settlements and reliable water sources.

Large industrial facilities are scattered throughout the Zone, particularly water extraction, purification, storage, and distribution facilities. Some of these complexes are enormous, with machinery built directly into the surrounding terrain.

Rho also has a large network of smaller settlements. Some are old communities that have existed for generations, while others grew around industrial facilities.

## HISTORY

Rho's earliest human settlements were established as prisoner and slave colonies, originally used for quarrying and extracting stone and construction materials. Over generations, their descendants developed into the **Talora**, forming a distinct culture and identity.

Water extraction eventually became Rho's dominant industry. As the industry expanded, Talora communities were gradually displaced by extraction facilities, pipelines, reservoirs, roads, and new settlements.

Rho has experienced periods of prosperity because of its water industry, but much of that wealth has historically remained concentrated around the companies and communities closest to the industry.

## CULTURE

Rho has a strong regional identity, and Rhoans generally take pride in their culture despite widespread stereotypes about the Zone.

Rhoan culture is influenced by frontier traditions, Southern influences, vehicle culture, and a strong emphasis on self-sufficiency. Rhoans tend to be skeptical of outsiders, particularly people from wealthier or more urban Zones.

Older technology and traditional customs are often embraced rather than discarded. Repairing, modifying, and reusing things is common, and practical skills are highly valued.

### Appearance

Rhoans commonly have tan to medium-brown skin, brown or dark brown hair, and soft facial features with relatively broad or flat proportions. Wavy and curly hair is common, as is facial hair among adults who grow it.

These traits vary considerably between individuals and are not universal among Rhoans.

### Fashion

Rhoan clothing tends to be **practical, durable, and loose-fitting**, particularly because of the climate and the Zone's industrial and agricultural lifestyle.

Workwear such as cargo pants, coveralls, utility vests, boots, gloves, and oversized jackets is common. Clothing is often modified with pockets, straps, tools, or other equipment.

Outside of workwear, Rhoan fashion has strong **streetwear and motorsport influences**. Graphic jackets, racing-inspired clothing, cropped tops, loose trousers, caps, and customized garments are common.

Bright colors, particularly **orange, red, yellow, and blue**, are popular as accents and statement pieces. Secondhand, repaired, and repurposed clothing is also widespread.

### Vehicle Culture

Vehicles are deeply embedded in Rhoan life. The distance between settlements makes them necessary, while racing and vehicle modification are popular forms of recreation.

Rho is known throughout [Zenith](/lore/zenith.html) for heavily modified vehicles, off-road racing, and unusual custom builds. Racing takes place both informally and through organized events, and major races and vehicle shows are frequently filmed in the Zone.

### Music

Rho has a recognizable musical tradition that is considered older and more traditional than the music of many urban Zones. Its continued popularity is partly attributed to Rho's isolation and the importance placed on preserving local culture.

### Food & Drink

Rhoan cuisine is heavily inspired by Southern American comfort food. Strong spices, sauces, rubs, and seasoning are common.

Fresh ingredients and home cooking are culturally important. Depending on the settlement, gardens, livestock, hunting, and other small-scale food production may supplement imported food.

Rho also has a strong culture of locally produced alcohol. Homemade drinks are often particularly strong and pungent.

### Sports & Festivals

**Dustball** is a popular Rhoan sport loosely descended from soccer. Different communities maintain their own variations.

The largest annual festival is **the Dustrink**, which combines Dustball, racing, food, music, drinking, and other local traditions. The festival is primarily community-run, and [Monolith](/lore/companies.html) involvement has historically been limited.

### Local Speech

Rhoan speech has its own regional slang.

- **Longhaul:** A long-distance trip, particularly between distant settlements.
- **Tin:** An old vehicle or piece of machinery.
- **Dry luck:** Bad luck or an unfortunate situation.
- **Company town:** A settlement dominated by [Monolith](/lore/companies.html) infrastructure and employment.
- **Pipe Rat:** Someone who works around or for the water Monolith. Can also be used dismissively toward Monolith employees.
- **Pipe Mouse:** The child of a Pipe Rat, or someone who grew up around the Monolith without working for it.
- **Dusting:** Driving or riding around without a particular destination.
- **Burnout:** Someone who spends most of their money on gambling, racing, and related hobbies.
- **Junkblood:** A self-proclaimed title used by people who build, repair, or modify things from scrap.

Some terms are also used against Rhoans by outsiders. **Rusty**, **Canyon Rat**, **Tanker**, and **Outback** are derogatory terms associated with stereotypes of Rhoans as poor, isolated, or backwards.

## DAILY LIFE

Most Rhoans live in small settlements rather than large cities. Daily life generally revolves around work, family, local businesses, and community activities.

Repairing vehicles, machinery, buildings, and household goods is common. Mechanics, fabricators, and other skilled tradespeople are important members of many communities.

Water availability varies between settlements. Larger cities and industrial communities generally have reliable supplies, while remote communities may depend on wells, storage systems, or water deliveries.

Bars, diners, garages, shops, and private homes commonly serve as gathering places.

Education is generally less well-funded than in wealthier Zones. Rho has several trade schools and one university in its main industrial area, although residents seeking specialized education often leave the Zone.

[Fixing](/lore/fixers.html) is also particularly common. Rho's isolation and limited law-enforcement coverage make it a common starting point and temporary base for Fixers.

## SUBZONES / DISTRICTS

### Industrial District

Rho's primary administrative and industrial center. It contains the largest concentration of [Monolith](/lore/companies.html) offices, corporate infrastructure, major water facilities, and industrial employment, along with a relatively small city.

### Badlands District

The largest and least-developed part of Rho. It consists primarily of barren wilderness, abandoned infrastructure, remote roads, isolated water sources, mines, workshops, and scattered settlements.

[Monolith](/lore/companies.html) services and [Gavel](/institutes/gavel.html) presence are more limited here.

### Historical District

The districts containing Rho's established towns and communities, including older settlements and newer communities built around industrial facilities.

## KEY LOCATIONS

- **The Industrial District:** Rho's largest concentration of corporate infrastructure, industry, businesses, and employment.
- **Major Water Facilities:** Large extraction, purification, storage, and distribution complexes operated by AquaFlo.
- **Frontier Towns:** Numerous small settlements spread throughout the Zone.
- **Dustball Grounds:** Local and regional sporting grounds found throughout the Zone.
- **Racing Grounds:** Organized tracks and open areas used for professional and amateur racing.


## CREATING A RHOAN CHARACTER

A character from Rho could come from a small frontier settlement, an industrial company town, or one of the Zone's larger communities.

Common backgrounds include water-industry workers, mechanics, racers, tradespeople, [Fixers](/lore/fixers.html), and people involved in local businesses.

Rhoans are often familiar with long-distance travel, vehicle maintenance, practical repair work, and making do with limited resources.

Someone from a wealthier or more urban Zone may find Rho's culture old-fashioned, while a Rhoan may see those same people as sheltered or overly dependent on corporate infrastructure.

## TRIVIA

- Rho is the largest source of processed water for the rest of [Zenith](/lore/zenith.html).
- Vehicle and racing programs frequently use Rho as a filming location because of its open terrain.
- Some Rhoan communities maintain buildings and machinery that have been repaired or modified for generations.
- Locally produced alcohol is often considered unusually strong by people from other Zones.
- Rho's major annual festival has historically remained independent of [Monolith](/lore/companies.html) sponsorship.
- Rhoans generally have a higher degree of practical mechanical and self-sufficiency skills than outsiders.
