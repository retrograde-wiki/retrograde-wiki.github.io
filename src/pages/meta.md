## ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Retrograde began on April 27th, 2023. Since then, it has undergone extensive changes, rewrites, and countless additions. What began as a much smaller project has grown into a far larger and more complex world, with plenty more to explore in the years ahead.

In its early stages, Retrograde never had a dedicated wiki. This website aims to change that, with the goal to gather years of artwork, lore, and worldbuilding into a single, accessible reference for the setting. Think of it as a sort of DM guide, A place to understand the world, its characters, and the corporations that shape it.

There have also been plans to explore Retrograde through short stories, comics, video games, and animatics. For now, the focus is currently on Retrograde as a TTRPG setting, one that will continue to grow, evolve, and occasionally be rewritten as new ideas take shape.

That is to say, anything on this website is subject to change.

Thank you for taking the time to view it!

## TO-DO's

The biggest current priority is adding all 50 characters. Each one is planned to have its own references and artwork, alongside their written profiles, so this will take some time. But it'll get done. 

Beyond that, there's still plenty of work to do on the setting itself. More Zone pages, Monolith pages, company profiles, institutes, and everything in between are all on the list.

For now, the goal is simply to get everything documented and onto the site.
