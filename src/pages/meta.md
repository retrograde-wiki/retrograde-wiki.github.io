## CREDITS

- Retrograde, as a Project and Website, was Written, designed and built by Retrograde on Neocities.
- Fonts: Tomorrow, IBM Plex Sans, Bebas Neue
- Any External Art or Photography is used as placeholder with full credits/links to their creator where the image is found.
- Built with HTML, CSS and Eleventy
