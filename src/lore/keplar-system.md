The **Keplar System** is a six-planet star system located beyond Sol. It was settled by humanity approximately 800 years ago following the abandonment of Earth, with the planet Zenith serving as humanity's new home.

Due to the loss of many advanced manufacturing capabilities over centuries, modern technology retains the appearance and design philosophy of "cassette futurism". 
# Star
## Keplar Prime

A stable orange **K-type** star that provides slightly warmer and dimmer light than Earth's Sun. 

# Planets

## I. Orus

A small, scorched planet closest to Keplar Prime. Its surface is mainly volcanic regions, mineral deserts, and mysterious impact scars. 

## II. Ceresa

A dry desert world with vast dune seas, rocky plateaus, and a thin atmosphere.

## III. Zenith

## IV. Thion

A massive gas giant with numerous moons. Visible from Zenith as a bright object in the night sky.

## V. Euclite

An ice giant surrounded by frozen moons and debris fields.

## VI. Lilon

 a frozen dwarf planet located at the edge of the system.
