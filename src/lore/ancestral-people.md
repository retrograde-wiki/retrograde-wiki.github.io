The Ancestral Peoples are the different cultures of Zenith of those descended from the original Zenith colonies.

Specifically, those who were not allowed access into zones, or who were abandoned after attempt at building a zone. 

Instead of moving into the zones with the others, The Ancestral people of different zones worked together to create their own communities to stand on their own, even with their own Barrier technology

The term "Ancestral Peoples" is used broadly to describe cultures that maintain a strong relationship with their homeland, preserving traditions passed down through generations. 

They are not a single ethnicity or civilization, and they are generally referred to by outsiders from whatever zone they ''inhabit''. 

They typically live on the outskirts, and whether they accept runaways or not depends on the group.
