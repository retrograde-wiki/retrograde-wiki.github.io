## OVERVIEW

The Vojari are a very scattered group, as they have no set homeland. They travel, typically in small moving homes- typically electrically powered, but sometimes pulled by large anomalies. These homes are incredibly armored, and nearly impenetrable, the sheer sturdiness being the only reason they're able to weather through the [Blood moons].

Sometimes a group of Vojari will travel together, especially family groups, but it's not uncommon to see one completely alone. Typically they tend to keep good relations with most other ancestral nations, often trading with, or even permanently moving in with them. 

Many of the Vojari depend on the trade of other nations as they cannot spend time in one place farming, they typically do not have electricity as all of it is used to power the moving homes, and they do not have large machinery needed to produce certain products.  
Another common reason the Vojari will come to other nations is for medical attention. While many Vojari understand basic medicine out of necessity, and own basic first aid supplies, they do not have the facilities or equipment needed for advanced surgeries, or needed to treat serious illnesses. 

While outsides are not typically allowed to join a Vojari family (unless through marriage or adaption), many are allowed to temporarily travel with a group. Typically outsiders will be taken in, and then dropped off at the nearest friendly nation that's willing to take the outsider in.

Typically an individual or group will settle in one spot for weeks to even months, before moving whenever anomaly activity in the area gets high. 

## CULTURE

Many Vojari rely on scavenging ruins, selling many of the valuables they find to other nations. Others focus herding domesticated anomalies for a living. Others are very talented crafts people who use natural resources to create most of the things they need.

Typically the sense of family is quite strong, as multiple generations typically will live within only a few homes and will spend a lot of time together. Generally families only ever split in the case of marriages, death, some choosing to settle, or a member being ex-communicated due to crimes against the family.

## Appearance 

There's no Vojari race, so members will typically look incredibly varied. Many are very pale though, due to not much natural sunlight getting into the moving homes. 

Clothes don't tend to have one look either. Although scavenger Vojari especially tend to wear more old fashioned clothes that they've found.
