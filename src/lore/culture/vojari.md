## OVERVIEW
The Talora are one of Zenith's Ancestral Peoples. They are native to the badlands and mesas of Zone 17 Rho. In particular, their biggest settlement is built into Rho's famous Blood Canyon.
## CULTURE
The Settlements of the Taloran people are built into the canyons and cliffs of the Blood canon. Specifically to get to their biggest civilation, Talora, you have to navigate to the very centers oasis. Due to the Canyons rough nature, its a good aberrant for corpos or anomalies- though they leave many bridges/stairways/lifts connecting each 'neighborhood'.

The Taloran heavily value their community, but are equally opening to any outsider who wishes to come visit. They are known for being friendly and eager to work with outsiders. This being said, they tend to be EXTREMELY adverse to those who bring harm to their grass/oasis, as its the land remaining space of Land that RhoGro hasnt got their hands on yet. 

Despite this, Many go around visiting those in the outskirts of Zone 17 to bring them water, food and clothes if theyre poor. This is to the point of their people being completely banned from setting foot near RhoGro, as theyve had a longstanding habit of fraternizing with their disgruntled employees and giving them water in the hot weather.
## APPEARANCE
The Talora, like most other Ancestral people, tend to have a wide arrange of appearances. Typically, they have warm-tan to brownishskin with warmer hues, with dark brown or red-tinted black hair with brown or hazel eyes. Men and women dress more practically and androgynous, and both usually have longer hair.
