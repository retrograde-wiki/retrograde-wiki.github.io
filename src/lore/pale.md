**The Pale** refers to the vast, uninhabitable regions located between the world's established [Zones](/zones/). Once inhabited, much of the Pale has become a toxic and dangerous wasteland unsuitable for permanent settlement.

The region is home to numerous **Anomalies**, environmental hazards, abandoned infrastructure, and other threats. 

Travel through the Pale is dangerous but often necessary. [Couriers, smugglers, Fixers](/lore/fixers/), and other independent workers frequently cross its territories to transport people and goods between [Zones](/zones/).

The Pale is largely outside the control and protection of the governments and Corporations that operate within the Zones.
