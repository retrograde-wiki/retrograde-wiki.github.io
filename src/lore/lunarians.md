**Lunarians** are a species of evolved humans descended from the original human population of Earth. Over generations of living on Zenith, Lunarians developed a number of physical and biological traits distinct from traditional humans. Despite these differences, they are generally considered part of humanity's evolutionary lineage.

# Biology and Appearance

Lunarians vary significantly in appearance. Unlike traditional humans, they **may possess skin/hair in nearly any color of the visible spectrum** , with coloration primarily inherited from their parents. 

Lunarians also possess **unusually colored eyes and blood**. An individual's eye and blood color are determined by their **Zodiac** sign and **Birthday**. Each Zodiac is associated with a specific coloration. 

For example, individuals born under [Arc](/lore/calender) possess yellow eyes and yellow blood. Other zodiac signs are associated with their own distinct colors, and even personality traits. It is not entirely understood why this happens.

Besides their coloring, **Lunarians also have pointed ears**, which are present in the vast vast majority of the species. The exact shape and length of the ears can vary between individuals.

Their heights can span much much higher and lower then humans as well.

They are also born from Gellatinous Eggs. Instead of being born at '1', they are born at '0', and spend the first year growing inside. **Weird?**


# Evolution

Lunarians originated from humans who left Earth and adapted to life on another world. Over an extended period of time, environmental conditions and generations of evolution caused the population to diverge significantly from their Earth-born ancestors.

The exact causes behind many modern Lunarian traits remain unkown. Some theories attribute their development to the environment of their adopted planet, while others point toward exposure to unusual forms of radiation and other conditions.

The term human may therefore refer either specifically to the original Earth population or broadly to both humans and Lunarians, depending on the context.
