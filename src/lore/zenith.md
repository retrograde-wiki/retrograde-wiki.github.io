Zenith is an Earth-like terrestrial planet located within the Keplar star system. After Earth became unlivable due to climate change, The (middle to upper class) humans of Earth were transferred to the planet Zenith in the Keplar solar system. Unfortunately everything on the planet, from the flora to fauna, are completely corrupt and ruthless. 

And so, the 24 Zones were established by the Conglomerate, Giving the people of Zenith a safe place to refuge.

Each of the 24 zones are named after a letter from the greek alphabet.
