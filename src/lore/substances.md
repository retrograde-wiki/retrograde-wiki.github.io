## Slough
Refined industrial waste, typically collected from the waterways around monolithic factories. Typically takes up to a week to fully distill, although many producers will attempt to dubiously speed up the process. Once prepared most units will be sold as an ineluctable fluid, although some may choose to sell the substance as a pill. 

The high is considered to be extremely euphoric, with drowsiness, hallucinations, and some loss of motor capabilities being expected. 

The most common negative side effect is necrosis near the site of injections, with infections being incredibly common. Organ failure, blood clotting, and other serious side effects are also common, leading this to be one of the most deadly drugs widely circulated.  

It's typically very cheap and easy to get, making it most commonly found in impoverished communities, but its also popular among children of all classes due to the aforementioned ease of access.

## Skip

Dependent on the dose Skip can either blur parts of someones memory, or wipe out entire chunks of time. Typically with therapy the effects can be reversed, but high enough doses or repeated use can permanently damage the brain, causing dementia like symptoms. 

Skip is a medically approved substance often used in therapeutic settings to "treat" PTSD, although its often sold over the counter in more sketchy pharmacies. It is also of course, commonly used by the monoliths and [The police faction].
