Unlike Earth's calendar, Zenith's months were designed around the planet's nine lunar traditions, creating a unique temporal system tied to astronomical observation.

A full Zenithian year contains:
- 9 months
- 54 weeks
- 378 days

Each month contains exactly:
- 42 days
- 6 weeks

Zenith, noteably has **nine moons**, all (sometimes) visible from the planet's surface. Each moon is associated with one of the nine zodiac signs.
One would say, "I was born under Iris".

## Climate & Seasons

Zenith experiences four major seasons influenced by the planet's axial tilt and orbital cycle. Seasons are unequal in length.

| Season | Length | Months | Sunrise | Sunset |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| Spring | 84 days | 2 months | 06:15 | 18:15 |
| Summer | 105 days | 1.5 months | 04:45 | 20:15 |
| Autumn | 84 days | 2 months | 06:15 | 18:15 |
| Winter | 105 days | 1.5 months | 07:45 | 16:15 |
