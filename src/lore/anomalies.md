# Anomalies

**Anomalies** are phenomena native to **Zenith** that cannot be fully explained or understood through science. The term "Anomaly" encompasses a wide variety of abnormal flora, fauna, chemicals, objects, and locations.

Anomalies vary significantly in appearance and behavior. Some are relatively harmless, while others pose extreme dangers to nearby people, infrastructure, and environments.

Most Anomalies are contained outside the [Zones](/zones/) by the barriers surrounding them. These defenses protect the public from the majority of known Anomalies and prevent them from entering populated areas.

However, particularly powerful or **high-energy Anomalies** are capable of breaching these barriers. When this occurs, the resulting incidents can cause significant damage and may require evacuation, containment, or intervention by [fixers](/lore/fixers/) or corporate personel.

[The Pale](/lore/pale/) contains the largest known concentration of Anomalies, making travel and permanent settlement outside the Zones particularly dangerous.
